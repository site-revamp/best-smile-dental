# Best Smile Dental — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Best Smile Dental is a Singapore-based neighbourhood dental clinic located in Upper Boon Keng, led by Dr Javious Lim. The clinic provides general, cosmetic, restorative, and specialist dental treatments, including orthodontics, dental implants, gum treatments, and advanced digital dentistry.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a modern, welcoming, and reassuring digital experience that highlights the clinic's commitment to gentle, affordable, and reliable dental care while simplifying treatment discovery and appointment enquiries.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Homepage Content Hierarchy

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage contained valuable information about dental services, technology, patient benefits, and the clinical team, but these elements competed for attention without a sufficiently clear content hierarchy.

**Redesign Improvement:** Reorganised the homepage into a more structured patient journey, prioritising the clinic's core message, treatment options, modern technology, dental team, and appointment pathways. This helps visitors understand the clinic's strengths and navigate essential information more efficiently.

### 2. Simplified Treatment Discovery and Navigation

**Previous Issue:** The original treatment presentation relied on numerous individual service links, making it harder for visitors to identify relevant dental care categories quickly.

**Redesign Improvement:** Organised treatments into five clear service categories: General Dentistry, Gum Care, Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry, Orthodontics, and Dental Implants. This patient-focused structure improves service visibility and helps visitors explore suitable treatments without unnecessary browsing.

### 3. More Consistent Appointment and Contact Journey

**Previous Issue:** Appointment booking and WhatsApp contact options needed greater consistency across the patient journey. Published opening hours also differed between existing website pages, creating potential confusion.

**Redesign Improvement:** Increased the prominence of booking and WhatsApp actions throughout the homepage, creating clearer pathways towards appointments and enquiries. Contact and opening-hours information is treated consistently, with conflicting schedules identified for verification before publication.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Established a more logical progression from the main introduction to treatments, clinical expertise, technology, and appointment actions.
- **Improved Service Organisation:** Grouped treatments into five patient-friendly categories for easier discovery and navigation.
- **Welcoming Visual Presentation:** Emphasised clean layouts, balanced spacing, and reassuring messaging consistent with the clinic's gentle-care positioning.
- **Stronger Trust Communication:** Highlighted Dr Javious Lim, the clinical team, patient-centred care, and modern dental technology.
- **Improved Appointment Accessibility:** Prioritised booking and WhatsApp contact options throughout the homepage.
- **Responsive User Experience:** Focused on readable content, accessible navigation, and convenient contact actions across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Consistent Brand Presentation:** Maintained the clinic's existing brand direction while improving typography, spacing, content organisation, and overall visual consistency.

## Summary

The redesigned Best Smile Dental homepage addresses three primary communication and usability challenges: competing homepage content, fragmented treatment discovery, and inconsistent appointment pathways.

The improvements create a clearer, more welcoming, and patient-focused experience that reinforces the clinic's gentle, affordable, and reliable approach to dentistry while helping visitors explore treatments and arrange appointments more conveniently.

**Pre-Launch Note:** Confirm the clinic's current operating hours across the homepage, booking page, and contact page before publishing the redesign.
