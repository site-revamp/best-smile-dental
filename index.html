Helpful information

Questions before your visit?

Here are a few useful starting points. Contact the clinic directly for advice about your own dental needs.

Where is Best Smile Dental located?

Best Smile Dental is at 11 Upper Boon Keng Road, #01-929, Singapore 380011. The clinic states that it is less than a five-minute walk from Kallang MRT.

What types of dental care are available?

The clinic lists general dentistry, gum care, wisdom tooth treatment, braces and clear aligners, cosmetic and restorative dentistry, and dental implants. The dentist can advise which options are suitable for your needs.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Yes. You can contact the clinic by WhatsApp at +65 8978 7051 or email contact@bestsmiledental.com.sg.

Are CHAS and MediSave available?

The clinic states that CHAS support and MediSave claim assistance may be available for eligible patients and selected treatments. Please confirm eligibility and applicable details directly with the clinic.

Can I get an urgent dental appointment?

The clinic indicates that same-day and emergency care may be arranged where appointment slots allow. Contact the clinic to check availability for your concern.
